# Privacy

IsometriX MVP is **local-first and account-free**.

- Workout history, preferences, XP, and avatar selections store in on-device SQLite.
- No analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or backend.
- No hidden network requests required for core workout loops.
- Export JSON/CSV and share via the system share sheet when you choose.
- Delete/reset clears user history and progress; seeded exercise content remains.
- Optional notifications use OS permission; denial keeps reminders off.
- Future cloud/Health Connect integrations must be explicit opt-in (see ROADMAP).
